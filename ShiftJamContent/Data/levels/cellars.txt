@name The Cellars
; THE CELLARS (26x66). A switchback: nine wide floors 6 rows apart, each with a 6-tile hole at alternating
; sides, so you walk across a band and drop to the next. Optional Astral shortcuts: 'w' floor patches in
; bands 2, 6, 7 let you drop early. Band 5 holds a mandatory 4-thick Material-only wall (x11-14) that has to
; be crossed in Astral. The last room (rows 55-63, floor row 64) is the Hollowed arena; the exit X is at its
; far (left) end, x2, row 63. Enemies: 4 Hollowed, 2 Flicker, 3 Shell, 2 Wraith (11).
