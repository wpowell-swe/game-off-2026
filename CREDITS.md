## How this game was made

William leads the design and decides what ships. Much of the code, the story and lore content (through a small
multi-agent writing pipeline in `pipeline/`), and some of the art were produced with AI tools, and then directed,
reviewed and edited by the team. Specifically:

- **Code:** written with the help of Claude (Anthropic).
- **Story and lore content:** drafted by LLM agents from the project's lore bible, then reviewed.
- **Title screen art:** generated with Stable Diffusion XL and the pixel-art-xl LoRA (below), then processed to a 32-color palette.
- **Sound effects and the icon:** generated by scripts in `tools/` (no recorded or sampled audio).
