# World

Lanternfall is a mountain town where paper lanterns are lit each dusk to guide wandering souls home. It has stone streets, a market, temple stairs and a bell tower.

The Material Plane is the solid world of bodies, doors, tools and food. The Astral Plane lies over it like a reflection: cold, quiet, full of drifting souls and memories. Most people never see it.

The Tithe is a collector who steals souls from Lanternfall. A stolen soul is torn into the Astral Plane while its body keeps walking, armed and obedient, as a Husk. Nobody is killed, which is what makes it so cruel.

Husks are hollowed townsfolk. A Husk's body is armoured in the Material Plane and its soul is exposed only in the Astral Plane. Freeing the soul returns the person to themselves, confused and grateful.

Wren is the last monk of the Quiet Hand, a small order whose secret art is walking in both planes at once. Wren is calm, dry-humoured and carries guilt about not warning the order in time.

Master Oss was Wren's teacher and the head of the Quiet Hand. The Tithe hollowed Oss first. Oss is stern, precise and proud of Wren even when armed against them.

The Quiet Hand's vow is: no one is left behind the Threshold. Wren repeats it before every fight.

Flickers are Husks whose souls pull them back and forth between the planes on a beat. A ring of light pulses just before each swap.

Wraiths are lost souls that have drifted too long in the Astral Plane. They harm only those who stand in that plane, and they mean no malice.

Ki is the monk's inner breath. It recovers in the Material Plane and drains in the Astral Plane, so a monk cannot linger there.

Rescued townsfolk gather in the Lanternfall square: Pell the lantern-keeper, Dova who runs the market stall, Brother Hask the bell-ringer, and Tamsin, a child who can hear the Astral Plane.

Charms are small relics carried by the freed. Each carries a shift effect that bends a rule of the planes slightly, such as a longer Astral stay or a wider Perfect Shift window.
