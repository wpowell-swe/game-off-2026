# World

Placeholder lore: edit freely. The retriever feeds the most relevant paragraphs to the agents, so keep each paragraph about one thing.

The Shift is the event that makes everything in the world swap between two states without warning. Nobody remembers the world before it.

Shifters are people who can choose when a swap happens, but only once per day.

Shift shards are fragments of the event; holding one lets an item trade places with a nearby object.
