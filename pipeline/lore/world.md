# World

Lanternfall is a mountain town where paper lanterns are lit each dusk to guide wandering souls home. It has stone streets, a market, temple stairs, deep cellars and a bell tower. The town's real talent is putting off problems until they become emergencies.

The Material Plane is the solid world of bodies, doors, tools and food. The Astral Plane lies over it like a reflection: cold, quiet, full of drifting souls and memories. Most people never see it and complain about the draught.

The Tithe is a collector who steals souls from Lanternfall. A stolen soul is torn into the Astral Plane while its body keeps working, armed and obedient. The Tithe is polite, patient and bureaucratic, and sends forms.

Hollowed are townsfolk whose bodies are armoured in the Material Plane and whose souls are exposed only in the Astral Plane. Joseph breaks the guard, shifts, and frees the soul. The freed are confused, grateful and a bit rude about it.

Shells are bodies with no soul left at all. They are empty, tireless and only exist in the Material Plane. They cannot see anyone standing in the Astral Plane.

Wraiths are lost souls that drifted too long in the Astral Plane with no body to return to. They harm only those who stand in that plane, and they mean no malice. Mostly they are lonely and moan about it.

Flickers are Hollowed whose souls pull them back and forth between the planes on a beat. A ring of light pulses just before each swap.

Joseph is the last monk of the Quiet Hand, a small order whose secret art is stepping between the Material and Astral Planes. Joseph is calm, deadpan and lazy in a way that has turned out to be expensive. Joseph saw the first signs of the Hollowing and did not report them, because it meant paperwork.

Master Oss was Joseph's teacher and the head of the Quiet Hand. The Tithe hollowed Oss first. Oss is stern, precise and unimpressed with Joseph even while armed against them.

The Quiet Hand's vow is: no one is left behind the Threshold. Joseph says it before every fight, grimly and a little sarcastically.

The Tithe's Clerk is the Tithe's enforcer and filing system. The Clerk moves between planes at will, stamps everything and never raises its voice.

Ki is the monk's inner breath. Stepping into the Astral Plane costs a lot of it, staying there costs a little, and it recovers in the Material Plane.

Each district of Lanternfall has a boss: a notable townsperson hollowed by the Tithe. The Lantern Streets are held by Pell the lantern-keeper, the Cellars by Dova who runs the market stall, the Temple Stairs by the Abbess, and the Bell Tower by Brother Hask the bell-ringer. The Tithe's Hall holds Master Oss and then the Clerk.

When Joseph frees a boss, that person walks to the Lanternfall square, confused and grateful, and gives Joseph a charm. Tamsin, a child who can hear the Astral Plane, waits in the square and is the only one honest about how bad things are.

The Abbess runs the Temple Stairs and fights from the Astral Plane. She is serene, severe and hollowed mid-sermon, and she is very hard to interrupt.

Charms are small relics carried by the freed. Each carries a shift effect that bends a rule of the planes slightly, such as cheaper shifting or a longer Astral stay.
