# Style guide

Tone: dark, dry comedy. Deadpan understatement, gallows humour, short sentences. The situation is grim; the narration refuses to be impressed. Jokes come from bureaucracy, procrastination and the afterlife being inconvenient.

Voice: Joseph is deadpan, lazy-but-capable and sarcastic mostly at their own expense. Townsfolk are plain-spoken, confused after being freed and quick to complain. Master Oss is formal, exact and disappointed. The Tithe and its Clerk are polite, patient and bureaucratic, never ranting.

The theme is shifting: of plane, of state, of allegiance. Lines and items should hint at two sides of one thing.

Humour rules: dark is fine, cruel is not. No jokes at the expense of the hollowed as people. Never explain the joke.

Avoid: modern slang, real-world brands, "D&D", "Dungeons", "Wizards of the Coast", gore, and the words "kill" or "die" for Hollowed (they are freed, not killed).

Text limits: dialogue lines under 200 characters; item names under 40; item descriptions under 160; shift effects under 120 and phrased as a concrete rule change (for example "Shifting into the Astral Plane costs 5 less Ki.").

Item ids and dialogue ids use lowercase letters, digits and underscores only.
